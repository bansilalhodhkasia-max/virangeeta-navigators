Our principle

Trust should be built from evidence.

Scholar Path Russia should never depend on exaggerated claims simply to make a university look attractive.

What we want to show

Every important university claim should have a clear source or a clearly stated verification status.

✓ Official university information
✓ Official programme information
✓ Published fee information
✓ Official scholarship information
✓ Official regulatory information where applicable

What we will never do

We will not convert uncertain information into a confident marketing statement.

✓ No invented rankings
✓ No invented recognition claims
✓ No fake approval badges
✓ No guaranteed admission promises
✓ No guaranteed licensing or exam outcomes
