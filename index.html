OUR PRINCIPLE

Trust should be built from evidence.

Scholar Path Russia should never depend on exaggerated claims simply to make a university look attractive.

What we want to show

Important university claims should have a clear source or clearly stated verification status.

  • Official university information
  • Official programme information
  • Published fee information
  • Official scholarship information
  • Official regulatory information where applicable

What we will never do

We will not convert uncertain information into confident marketing statements.

  • No invented rankings
  • No invented recognition claims
  • No fake approval badges
  • No guaranteed admission promises
  • No guaranteed licensing or exam outcomes
Verification status matters.

A field marked “Not verified” means Scholar Path Russia has not represented that information as verified. Always open the official source before making a decision.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Important questions, answered clearly.

Short answers for students and parents.

What does Scholar Path Russia provide? +

Scholar Path Russia is designed as a student decision platform for researching Russian universities, programmes, costs, scholarships, cities, documents and application pathways.

Are scholarships guaranteed? +

No. Scholarship eligibility, selection and availability depend on the relevant official programme and current rules.

Can university fees change? +

Yes. Fees and other costs can change. The database therefore displays verification status and official sources wherever available.

Is information about medical recognition permanent? +

No assumption should be made from a general website statement. Students should verify the current requirements of the relevant regulator before choosing a medical programme.

Why does Scholar Path Russia show verification notes? +

Because transparent uncertainty is better than an invented certainty. If something has not been verified, the website should say so.

Can I contact Scholar Path Russia for application guidance? +

Yes. Use the contact and application form below. The final form connection will be configured in the JavaScript file.
