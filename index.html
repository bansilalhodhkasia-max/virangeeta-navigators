Our principle

Trust should be built from evidence.

Scholar Path Russia should never depend on exaggerated claims simply to make a university look attractive.

What we want to show

  • Official university information
  • Official programme information
  • Published fee information
  • Official scholarship information
  • Official regulatory information where applicable
  • Source and verification date wherever practical

What we will never do

  • No invented rankings
  • No invented recognition claims
  • No fake approval badges
  • No guaranteed admission promises
  • No guaranteed licensing or examination outcomes
  • No presenting outdated information as current

Frequently asked questions

Important questions, answered clearly.

Short answers for students and parents.

What does Scholar Path Russia provide?

A student-focused platform for researching, comparing and understanding Russian universities, programmes, costs, scholarships and related admission information.

Are scholarships guaranteed?

No. Scholarship availability, eligibility, selection and coverage depend on the applicable official programme and current rules.

Can university fees change?

Yes. Fees and other conditions can change by academic year. Students should verify current official information before making a decision.

Is medical recognition permanent?

Recognition and professional eligibility can depend on the country, programme, graduation year and current regulations. Always verify with the relevant official authority.

Why does Scholar Path Russia show verification notes?

Because important education claims should be traceable to an appropriate official or published source whenever possible.
